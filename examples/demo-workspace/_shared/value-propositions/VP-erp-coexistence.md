# Shop-floor depth without replacing the ERP

## Challenge
IT wants fewer vendors; operations wants a shop-floor system that plant staff actually use.

## Offer
Larkspur MES next to the existing ERP, connected through the standard ERP connector.

## Value
plant-floor adoption × productivity effect, against the integration cost of a second vendor.

## Proof
None recorded.

## Fit conditions
ERP stays the system of record for orders and materials.

## Gaps and risks
Relies on the standard ERP connector, which product material lists as announced, not available.
