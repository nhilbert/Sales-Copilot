# Guaranteed response times with a service-level credit

## Challenge
After a performance problem, operations no longer trusts the system and finance questions the contract value.

## Offer
A renewal with measured response-time targets per plant and a service-level credit if they are missed.

## Value
risk of production delays priced as a credit × probability; for finance, a contract that pays back when it underperforms.

## Proof
None yet. Requires agreement from delivery and finance on our side.

## Fit conditions
Root cause understood and a fix in sight; measurement agreed with the client's IT.

## Gaps and risks
Commercial approval for service-level credits is not standard; check with commercial management before offering.
