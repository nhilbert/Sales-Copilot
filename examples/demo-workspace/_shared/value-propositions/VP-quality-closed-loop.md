# Close the loop between quality deviations and production

## Challenge
Deviations are recorded, but nobody sees which production orders they affect, and corrective actions are not followed through.

## Offer
Larkspur Quality CAPA workflow linked to production orders in Larkspur MES.

## Value
recurring deviations avoided × cost per deviation (rework, scrap, audit findings). Use the client's own deviation statistics.

## Proof
Existing quality installation; linkage to MES not yet shown at a client.

## Fit conditions
Quality module in use or planned; MES in use or planned in the same plant.

## Gaps and risks
Value depends on MES being present; without MES, offer the CAPA workflow alone.
