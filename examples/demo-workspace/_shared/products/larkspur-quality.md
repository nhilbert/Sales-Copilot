# Larkspur Quality (fictional example product)

## What it is
Quality management software: deviations, corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), audits, supplier complaints.

## Capabilities
- Deviation and CAPA workflow across plants — generally available — factsheet 2026-06
- Audit planning and checklists — generally available — factsheet 2026-06
- Mobile audit app (offline) — announced for Q2 2027 — roadmap deck 2026-07
- Link deviations to production orders (requires Larkspur MES) — generally available — factsheet 2026-06

## Typical buyers and problems
Head of Quality (recurring deviations, audit findings), plant managers (scrap, rework), purchasing (supplier quality).

## Not offered / limits
No laboratory information management; supplier portal only via partner.
